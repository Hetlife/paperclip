# Species legality check

**No species may be quoted, ordered, or shipped without a `VERIFIED` row
signed off by a named human.** An agent may fill the research columns
and propose a status, but may never move a row to `VERIFIED`.

Scope reminder: captive-bred **freshwater** fish and aquatic plants
only. Marine, wild-caught, CITES-listed, WLPA-scheduled, reptiles, and
invertebrates are all out of scope and should not appear here at all.

## Status values

- `UNVERIFIED` — research done or not, no human sign-off. Default.
- `FLAGGED` — research suggests a restriction. Do not pursue without
  counsel.
- `VERIFIED-CLEAR` — human/counsel confirmed permissible to export
  India → US. Requires name and date.
- `VERIFIED-BLOCKED` — confirmed restricted. Keep the row so nobody
  re-researches it later.

## Sources each row must be checked against

1. **WLPA schedules** (as amended 2022) — MoEFCC
2. **CITES appendices** — cites.org, current version
3. **US injurious wildlife list** — USFWS, 50 CFR 16
4. **APHIS / USFWS import requirements** — health certificate and
   permit conditions for live freshwater fish
5. **MPEDA guidance** on permitted ornamental export species

## Notes for whoever fills this

- Scientific names matter more than common names. The same common name
  covers different species across suppliers, and a mis-declaration is
  the specific failure mode that creates Lacey Act exposure.
- "Captive-bred" must be confirmed at the supplier, with documentation
  you could show a US importer. A supplier's verbal assurance is not
  documentation.
- If a species is ambiguous, it is `FLAGGED`, not `UNVERIFIED`. Ambiguity
  is information.
